    • 9048-Independent explorations - IA3 - Imaginative response

      Reviewed by Mrs Linda Carroll in Literature [11-LIT11-B] | 1:50pm

      25 / 25

      FINAL: Creepy and sinister with just the right amount of intrigue to maintain credibility.  The narrative defies reader expectations and yet does not disappoint as the ending is not predictable.

      Lovely attention to the subtleties of loss and grief and the wobbly, imperfect memories that he is clinging to. 

      Written with careful attention to detail.  The only possible flaw is the crashing noise made by the loft ladder when he finally opens it - why hadn't he heard this during the night?

      DRAFT: This a great start and has some lovely language and expression to engage the reader with a subtle and careful attention to detail.

      Structurally, there can be some deliberate adjustments to mark the points of change and shifts in focus - there also needs to be a more attentive control of paragraphing conventions.

      The time and setting is confusing - where is this and why has this setting been chosen?  Does this man work - how old is he - how did she die?  Are these things important and would they influence changes to the story?  he seems young but does nothing - but he can climb into an attic so perhaps agile.  The agent appears to hint at a haunted home but the ghost is his own wife - how did this happen? Review plan for our next chat.
